## International decision

The six fully translated discovery routes have seven stable language addresses: English without a prefix, French/Spanish/German/Italian/Portuguese/Dutch with a prefix. They self-canonicalize and link reciprocally with hreflang, including x-default to English. Cookies and Accept-Language do not change their document language. The static sitemap contains 42 discovery URLs and eight existing editorial URLs.

Profiles, media, individual collections and functional pages retain one bare public URL. Their existing language preference still applies. They do not advertise seven duplicate URLs to crawlers. The language switcher preserves queries/fragments and writes the preference before navigation. Homepage title/description overrides and all dictionaries remain unchanged.

## Acceptance checks and deployment order

1. Monitor: exactly two video pages and one photo page selected from active restored records. At most eight requests per run, bounded Range reads and timeouts, no redirects/retries, no writes to catalogue/storage. A truncated page, stale report or failed request cannot count as healthy. Retired pages must not be fetched as assets. Report contains aliases and technical statuses, never URLs/secrets. Timer every 15 minutes; independent of repair/ingestion services.
2. Maison Studio: display monitor health and coverage alongside existing bot/repair alerts. Stale or absent measurements are unknown, not a green full-library assurance. Do not change bot control behavior.
3. Models: real previous/next anchors in server HTML, selected sort and page retained, canonical to the actual page, provider bounds respected. Keep the existing load-more control.
4. Performance: compare the same mobile Chromium throttling profile before/after. The actual observed LCP images must stop being lazy loaded; elevate at most the first two main thumbnails per grid. Leave later thumbnails lazy and videos preload=none. Record synthetic limitations and absence of GSC field data.
5. Search: record property, date range, filters and freshness. Distinguish historical alerts from current HTTP/HTML checks. No submissions, changes to noindex en masse, CTR changes or ranking guarantees.
6. Run targeted and combined Node/Python tests, typecheck/lint and a production build. Review the connected changes before committing.
7. Deploy only clean committed archives. Candidate must pass locale/canonical/hreflang/pagination, media/withdrawal guards, saved-copy equality, readiness and consent smoke checks. Preserve app environment, DB container and edge configuration. Retain the old app and configuration for rollback. Deploy monitor/controller/UI independently without restarting repair workers or megabots.
